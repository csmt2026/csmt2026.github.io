About

I know what it takes to grow a brand and I know what kills one.

12 years of lived experience across every stage of the journey.

I am Manav. I co-founded LoveRaw and spent 12 years scaling it from my kitchen to one of the UK’s fastest-growing plant-based chocolate brands.

I secured listings in Tesco, Asda, Waitrose, Boots, WHSmith, and Co-Op in the UK, and expanded internationally into Carrefour, Spar, and Amazon across 25+ countries. I grew brand awareness from 5% to 18% in a single year, generated over 36 million media impressions, led multi-million-pound fundraising rounds, and brought in over £10 million in cumulative revenue.

I also lived the hard side. Product recalls. Trademark disputes. Global supply chain disruption. I have seen the full picture and that is exactly why founders trust me.

I now work with food and FMCG brands as a strategic advisor, helping founders scale faster and smarter than I did, while avoiding the mistakes I made along the way. I see the things you cannot see yourself, the hidden problems, the blind spots, the opportunities you are missing.
