What I Do

10 Core Areas

I work with ambitious food and FMCG brands across ten core areas, bringing hard-won experience from building, scaling, nearly losing, and rebuilding.

  1. 01

    Retail & Distribution Strategy

    I secured listings in Tesco, Asda, Waitrose, Boots, WHSmith, Co-Op, Carrefour, Spar, and Amazon. I help brands break into Tier 1 retailers, nail buyer pitches, optimise pricing, and build commercial relationships that actually last.

  2. 02

    Fundraising & Investor Readiness

    I led multi-million-pound fundraising rounds and sat across the table from angels, VCs, and PE firms. I help founders build investor narratives, sharpen financial models, target the right investors, and prepare for due diligence.

  3. 03

    Brand Strategy & Growth

    I grew LoveRaw’s awareness from 5% to 18% in one year, generated 36M+ media reach, and built a brand that owned 5 of the top 7 bars in its category. I help founders sharpen messaging, build community, and create brand love that converts.

  4. 04

    Building Your Economic Moat

    Scaling is one thing. Making your business defensible is another. I help founders build structural advantages: category ownership, IP protection, supply chain exclusivity, and brand loyalty that compounds over time.

  5. 05

    NPD & Innovation Pipeline

    I built a 3-year innovation pipeline and developed SKUs that led their segments in taste and performance. I help brands choose which products to back, validate them fast, and position them for both retailers and consumers.

  6. 06

    International Expansion

    I expanded LoveRaw into 25+ countries, secured a worldwide distribution agreement with Spar, and recruited international GMs. I help brands grow their global footprint without diluting focus or product-market fit.

  7. 07

    Wealth Preservation & Capital Efficiency

    I increased gross margins through smart cost-cutting and pricing, and I learned the hard way what happens when founders do not separate personal wealth from business risk. I help founders build financial resilience alongside growth.

  8. 08

    Operational Resilience & Crisis Management

    Product recalls. Trademark disputes. Global supply chain disruption. Administration. I have navigated all of it. I help founders create contingency plans, build supply chain resilience, and make sharp decisions under pressure.

  9. 09

    Sustainability & Mission-Led Business

    I achieved 41% lower emissions versus dairy chocolate, developed a clean-label ethical product range, and embedded ESG into product, brand, and team values. I help brands build purpose into operations. No greenwashing, just smart sustainability.

  10. 10

    Founder Mentorship & Productivity

    I grew a global brand while raising 4 young children. I have navigated overwhelm, identity shifts, and the pressure of being the person everyone depends on. I mentor founders with practical tools for time, energy, and decision-making.
