About

I know what it takes to grow a brand and I know what kills one.

12 years of lived experience across every stage of the journey.

I am Manav. I co-founded LoveRaw and spent 12 years scaling it from my kitchen to one of the UK’s fastest-growing plant-based chocolate brands.

I secured listings in Tesco, Asda, Waitrose, Boots, WHSmith, and Co-op in the UK, and expanded internationally into Carrefour, Spar, and Amazon across 25+ countries. I grew brand awareness from 5% to 18% in a single year, generated over 36 million media impressions, led multi-million-pound fundraising rounds, and brought in over £10 million in cumulative revenue.

I also lived the hard side. Product recalls. Trademark disputes. Global supply chain disruption. I have seen the full picture and that is exactly why founders trust me.

I now work with food and FMCG brands as a strategic advisor, helping founders scale faster and smarter than I did, while avoiding the mistakes I made along the way. I see the things you cannot see yourself, the hidden problems, the blind spots, the opportunities you are missing.

My story · The LoveRaw journey

It started in my kitchen.

No investors. No retail connections. No industry experience. Just a belief that plant-based chocolate could taste as good as dairy, and a willingness to figure it out as I went. That was 2013. My co-founder and I made products by hand and sold them at local markets.

Within a few years, we had our first retail listing. Then another. Then Tesco came calling. Then Asda, Waitrose, Boots, WHSmith and Co-op. Each one felt like an impossible mountain until we climbed it.

The brand grew fast. I took it international and joined the Tesco Incubator Scheme, and we came to own 5 of the top 7 bars in our UK category. I did all of this while raising four young children.

But it was not a perfect journey. I learned brutal lessons about cash flow, about protecting personal wealth from business risk, and about what happens when growth outpaces the infrastructure beneath it.

Ultimately, the business entered administration.

That is not something I shy away from. It is one of the most important things I bring to the table. The brand did not disappear: it was acquired out of administration and lives on today. I know exactly where the landmines are because I stepped on most of them.

I am not here to give you textbook advice. I am here because I have done the thing you are trying to do.
